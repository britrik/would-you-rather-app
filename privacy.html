Privacy Policy

VibingFun does not collect any personally identifiable information from users. No registration is required to use the app.

We use the self-hosted open-source tool tarteaucitron.js to show a consent banner with a separate opt-in toggle for each service — currently Google AdSense (advertising). No advertising script runs until you allow it — the only thing set beforehand is this tool's own first-party preference cookie (named tarteaucitron, holding your not-yet-made choice), which never leaves your browser. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time via the cookie icon or by opening your cookie settings. Your choice is stored locally in a preference cookie named tarteaucitron; no consent data is sent to us. Google AdSense, when allowed, may use cookies to serve ads, in accordance with Google’s ad policy.

For any privacy-related questions, contact us via the email or GitHub link on the home page.
